## 5. Finding classification

Classify findings from documented impact and evidence. Critical findings require immediate escalation and block readiness closure unless formally controlled by the authorized authority. High findings require prioritized remediation. Medium/low findings follow the approved corrective-action process.

## 6. Remediation requirements

Every material finding should have:

- unique reference;
- evidence source;
- owner role;
- corrective action;
- target date;
- re-test method;
- approval/disposition;
- closure evidence.

A finding cannot be closed merely because an action was attempted.

## 7. Continuity readiness decision

After remediation and re-testing, the authorized reviewer may record:

- `EXERCISE_ACCEPTED`
- `EXERCISE_ACCEPTED_WITH_ACTIONS`
- `REMEDIATION_REQUIRED`
- `EXERCISE_REPEATED_REQUIRED`
- `CONTINUITY_READINESS_BLOCKED`

These are governance states, not current production claims.

## 8. Lessons-to-control linkage

Material lessons should be linked to the appropriate control area:

- security/access;
- backup/recovery;
- monitoring;
- incident response;
- service continuity;
- change management;
- privacy/data protection;
- operational ownership.

Where a lesson results in a preventive-control change, route it through B.6.25–B.6.27 and applicable production authorization gates.

## 9. Evidence protection

Exercise and recovery evidence should remain in approved private systems. Use synthetic/non-production data where possible. Never store credentials, access tokens, connection strings or unnecessary personal data in the public repository.
